# Employee expense policy — sample corpus (public portfolio demo)

Policy ID: HR-EXP-331
Effective Date: March 1, 2025

Employees may submit expenses for pre-approved business travel.
Meal allowance is capped at $75 per day.
Lodging requires receipts for amounts above $120 per night.

Invoice Total example for training materials: $1,250.00

Approval chain:
- Manager for amounts under $500
- Finance for amounts of $500 or more

Expense reports must be filed within 14 days of travel completion.
