# Product warranty policy — sample corpus (public portfolio demo)

Policy ID: POL-2048-WX
Effective Date: 2024-01-15

Warranty Period: 24 months from the date of purchase for manufacturing defects.

Coverage:
- Hardware failure under normal use
- Replacement of defective components
- Software patches related to covered defects

Exclusions:
- Damage from misuse or liquid exposure
- Unauthorized modifications
- Consumable parts after normal wear

Claims Process:
1. Contact support with Policy ID POL-2048-WX
2. Provide proof of purchase
3. Allow remote diagnostics before RMA

Support contact: support@example-portfolio.demo
